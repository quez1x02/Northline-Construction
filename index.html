03 — A place in the neighbourhood

Made for the
in-between.

Morrow & Finch began with a window, a second-hand espresso machine and the idea that an everyday café can still feel special.

We work with small roasters, local growers and bakers who care about the details. Mostly, we make a warm room for the moments that don’t need a plan.
